Origami Reader: software components
===================================

Origami Reader is built with software made by others. Each component keeps
its own license; Origami's licenses (LICENSE and APP-LICENSE.md) do not
cover them and do not take away any right their licenses give you. The full
license texts and copyright notices of the JavaScript packages and Rust
crates are in licenses/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt in the installation folder
(Settings, About and support, Copyright and licenses, Open the licenses
folder). Python packages keep their notices in python/Lib/site-packages and
python-light/Lib/site-packages, in each package's .dist-info folder.

RUNTIMES SETUP INSTALLS
-----------------------

Python, by the Python Software Foundation. PSF License Agreement
  (python/LICENSE.txt).
PyTorch and torchaudio, by the Linux Foundation and contributors (Qwen
  speech). BSD 3-Clause.
  The PyTorch build includes Intel OpenMP (libiomp5md.dll), under Intel's
  license.
NVIDIA CUDA runtime and cuDNN libraries, inside the NVIDIA build of PyTorch
  (only on computers with an NVIDIA card). NVIDIA CUDA Toolkit EULA and cuDNN
  Software License Agreement: https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/eula/
GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 libraries, loaded as separate,
  replaceable files: libsoxr (in soxr) and libsndfile (in soundfile) for
  speech, and num2words for the light reader. You may replace them with your
  own builds. Source: https://github.com/chirlu/soxr ,
  https://github.com/libsndfile/libsndfile ,
  https://github.com/savoirfairelinux/num2words
Other Python packages for speech (transformers, qwen-tts and their
  dependencies): Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD and similar, each in its .dist-info
  folder.
stable-diffusion.cpp and ggml, by leejet and the ggml authors (pictures).
  MIT. Downloaded from the stable-diffusion.cpp GitHub releases. Its
  libwebp and libwebm libraries are by Google, BSD 3-Clause.
llama.cpp, by the ggml authors (Studio speaker finding). MIT. Downloaded
  from the llama.cpp GitHub releases.

The light reader (workers/light_tts.py) is a separate program under the GNU
General Public License v3 or later. It uses phonemizer-fork and espeak-ng
(through espeakng-loader), both GNU GPL 3.0 or later, installed in
python-light. See "Light reader" in Settings and
workers/LIGHT-READER-SOURCE.txt for their source.
