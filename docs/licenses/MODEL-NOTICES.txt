Origami Reader: models and voices
=================================

The AI models Origami Reader installs or downloads are made by others and keep
their own licenses. Origami's licenses (LICENSE and APP-LICENSE.md) do not
cover them and do not take away any right their licenses give you. Where
Origami installs a model, a copy of its license or notice is placed beside the
model files. The full texts of the Apache License 2.0, the CreativeML
OpenRAIL-M license and the Stability AI Community License are also in Settings,
About and support, Copyright and licenses.

Models are downloaded from their publishers (mostly Hugging Face) at fixed
revisions and checked against a SHA-256 fingerprint before use. "Unmodified"
below means the published files are used as they are.

  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M

voice library) are synthetic voices designed by Sov3ryn with Qwen VoiceDesign
from written descriptions. They are not recordings of real people and were not
made to imitate anyone. They are part of the app and covered by APP-LICENSE.md.

WHAT YOU MAKE
-------------

Narration, voices, pictures, speaker attributions and transcriptions are made
by AI models and can be wrong. Studio exports are labelled as AI-generated
(the "Made with Origami Reader" credit, AI metadata and, for audio, an
AudioSeal watermark). The models' licenses, including their use restrictions,
apply to what you make with them.
