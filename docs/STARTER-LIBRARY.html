Origami · starter library

Six complete books

Selections for variety, narrative appeal and manageable starting points. Lead with The Hound of the Baskervilles for suspense, The Time Machine for a short first listen, or The Blue Castle for warmth and romance.

01
Fantasy

The Enchanted Castle

E. Nesbit · 1907

A magic ring turns a holiday adventure into increasingly strange trouble. Funny, imaginative and occasionally eerie, with a low barrier to entry. This selection leans toward younger readers.

12 chapters · 68k words · about 7.5 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
02
Science fiction

The Time Machine

H. G. Wells · 1895

A short, unsettling journey into humanity's distant future. Its mysteries and survival scenes provide an immediate hook, while its ideas give readers something to think about afterward.

12 chapters · 32k words · about 3.6 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
03
Detective mystery

The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1902

Sherlock Holmes, an apparently supernatural curse and a dangerous investigation on the moors. A strong standalone introduction to Holmes, with suspense that should work particularly well aloud.

15 chapters · 59k words · about 6.6 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
04
Romance

The Blue Castle

L. M. Montgomery · 1926

A woman who has spent her life obeying an oppressive family decides to live on her own terms. Warm, witty and romantic, with a satisfying emotional arc and approachable prose.

45 chapters · 68k words · about 7.6 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
05
Realistic social drama

The Great Gatsby

F. Scott Fitzgerald · 1925

Wealth, extravagant parties, reinvention and obsession, followed by consequences. The shortest full-length novel in the group and a different, darker emotional register. Set in the 1920s, not the present day.

9 chapters · 48k words · about 5.4 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
06
Historical adventure

The Count of Monte Cristo

Alexandre Dumas · 1844

Betrayal, imprisonment, escape, hidden wealth and an elaborate revenge. The substantial surprise selection, chosen for its momentum and intrigue. The nineteenth-century English translation is older in style than modern commercial translations.

117 chapters · 460k words · about 51.2 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
