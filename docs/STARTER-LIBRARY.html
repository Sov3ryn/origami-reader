Six complete books

Six complete English editions selected for pay-what-you-want distribution. Try Journey to the Center of the Earth for adventure, Pride and Prejudice for dialogue, or the modern story collection for a shorter first listen.

01
Fantasy

The Enchanted Castle

E. Nesbit · 1907

A magic ring turns a holiday adventure into increasingly strange trouble. Funny, imaginative and occasionally eerie, with a low barrier to entry. This selection leans toward younger readers.

12 chapters · 68k words · about 7.5 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
02
Science fiction

Journey to the Center of the Earth

Jules Verne · 1864

An eccentric professor, his nephew and their guide descend through an Icelandic volcano into a subterranean world. An adventurous introduction to classic science fiction, using the older Malleson translation rather than a modern copyrighted one.

45 chapters · 73k words · about 8.1 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
03
Detective mystery

The Moonstone

Wilkie Collins · 1868

A stolen diamond, an isolated household and conflicting witnesses build a mystery told through memorable narrators. A foundational detective novel with humor, suspense and strong opportunities for listening aloud.

49 chapters · 195k words · about 21.6 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
04
Romance

Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen · 1813

Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy misjudge each other while navigating family pressure, pride and social expectations. Sharp dialogue and humor make this a strong romance selection for both reading and listening.

61 chapters · 122k words · about 13.5 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
05
Contemporary stories

Grayson's Book of Stories

Grayson Bray Morris · Online collection reviewed in 2026; individual stories have earlier publication dates

Sixteen modern stories range from everyday relationships and loss to robots, memory and speculative worlds. Short pieces offer manageable first listening sessions. Includes adult themes; this is a contemporary collection, not a present-day novel.

16 stories · 40k words · about 4.5 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
06
Historical adventure

The Count of Monte Cristo

Alexandre Dumas · 1844

Betrayal, imprisonment, escape, hidden wealth and an elaborate revenge. The substantial surprise selection, chosen for its momentum and intrigue. The nineteenth-century English translation is older in style than modern commercial translations.

117 chapters · 460k words · about 51.2 hours aloud
Open EPUBSource edition
