## Crane generation prompt

Extract and recreate only the folded origami paper crane logo from the top left of the approved library mockup. Isolated app brand asset on transparent square canvas. Match the tall sharply pointed raised right wing, broad diagonally raised left wing, folded central diamond body and slender right-facing neck and beak. Lavender and pale lilac facets, smoky violet shadows, crisp polygon edges, no outline, external shadow, text or badge. Keep the reference proportions and approximately ten percent transparent padding. Remain readable at 40 to 64 pixels.

Scene sources are kept as PNG in assets/scenes-source and are not bundled. `python scripts/optimize-scenes.py` writes the shipped WebP files (quality 95, measured at 42 to 45 dB PSNR). Day and night counterparts that are not painted yet (summer-day, autumn-day, winter-night) use a filtered copy of their pair as temporary draft art until a PNG with the same composition is added and converted.

Garden scenes added 15 October 2026 use the existing user-reviewed images from outputs/Origami-Garden-Concept: garden-spring-local-edit.png and garden-{summer,autumn,winter}-day.png. Their shipped public/backgrounds/garden-*.webp copies are lossless encodings at the original 1672 by 941 size. No additional image generation or restoration was applied. The four night scenes are explicitly labelled draft art and use filtered daytime images until dedicated night paintings exist. GardenEffects adds aligned mist, shadows, fireflies, a lantern, and a slow bamboo fountain using transparent SVG/CSS layers. Seasonal particles reuse the established renderer; motion respects hidden-window and reduced-motion settings.

The image manager exposes SD 1.5 + LCM, DreamShaper 8 LCM, SDXL-Lightning, and Z-Image-Turbo. DreamShaper retains the native Vulkan/CPU runner; the other three use the isolated, offline workers/image_worker.py adapter. A job exits to release its model. No model weights, personal runtime paths, or new download host are shipped in source. Per-model license labels are identification, not a substitute for the full notices and acceptance flow needed when download packages are published.
