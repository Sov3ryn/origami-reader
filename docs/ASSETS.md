# Asset provenance

- assets/branding/crane-master.png: AI-generated faceted lavender paper crane recreated from the approved library concept using the built-in image generation tool. Replaced the initial simplified vector after user feedback.
- public/crane.png: small raster export from that master for the interface.
- public/moonlit-lake.png: original AI-generated reading background created with the built-in image generation tool on 12 October 2026. No external source image was used. Included with the app's MIT grant to the extent rights are held by the contributors.
- src-tauri/icons: raster exports from the crane master, generated by the Tauri icon tool.
- src/seeds.ts: original short demonstration passages. Titles and author names are fictional UI examples, not imported published books.
- docs/Origami-Library.png and docs/Origami-Reader.png: original AI-generated design concepts, not screenshots of the implemented app. Their decorative text is not a product-copy specification.
- docs/screenshots: screenshots of the implemented interface.

Voice references, generated personal speech and model weights are excluded from the public repository. The MIT code license does not grant rights to someone else's voice pack or model.

## Background generation prompt

Original atmospheric background artwork for a premium desktop reading app called Origami. No text, no logo, no user interface. Wide composition with a moonlit lake, charcoal plum and midnight indigo, distant layered mountains, restrained moon reflection and a small warm window across the water. Plum blossom branches frame the edges. Low contrast through the center, painterly illustration with folded-paper-like distant planes. Quiet background for a reading surface, no people or borders.

## Crane generation prompt

Extract and recreate only the folded origami paper crane logo from the top left of the approved library mockup. Isolated app brand asset on transparent square canvas. Match the tall sharply pointed raised right wing, broad diagonally raised left wing, folded central diamond body and slender right-facing neck and beak. Lavender and pale lilac facets, smoky violet shadows, crisp polygon edges, no outline, external shadow, text or badge. Keep the reference proportions and approximately ten percent transparent padding. Remain readable at 40 to 64 pixels.
