Origami starter library, version 3

Gothic horror: Dracula, Bram Stoker.
Science fiction: Journey to the Center of the Earth, Jules Verne,
  F. A. Malleson's 1877 English translation.
Detective mystery: The Moonstone, Wilkie Collins.
Romance: Pride and Prejudice, Jane Austen.
Contemporary collection: Grayson's Book of Stories, Grayson Bray Morris.
Surprise adventure: The Count of Monte Cristo, Alexandre Dumas,
  anonymous Chapman and Hall English translation, 1846.

Complete text editions, with source art and fonts excluded. The modern
collection contains sixteen stories and includes adult themes and sexual
references. It is not a present-day novel or a dedicated YA collection.
The app preloads these six editions with bundled original cover art once.
Existing imports, custom covers, notes and reading positions are preserved.
Legacy demos are backed up locally before removal. Deleted starter entries
are not restored on later launches. Import books/*.epub for a separate copy.

No selected text has an NC-only license. Paid and zero-price distribution
is the intended use, subject to the notices in RIGHTS.txt. Older texts meet
the recorded age criteria, and Standard Ebooks contributions are CC0. The
modern collection remains CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution, source/license
notices and applicable ShareAlike terms. App code is under Origami's own license.

The economic-term review is broader than the earlier US/Canada/UK review,
but local public-domain fees and other requirements prevent an unqualified
worldwide clearance claim. Read RIGHTS.txt and international-review.json.
Release packaging requires --release --country=CODE. A development ZIP
is not a worldwide installer clearance. Translations and language-specific
selections remain later work with separate rights checks.
