Origami: six starter books

1. Fantasy: The Enchanted Castle, E. Nesbit
   Magic goes wrong during a holiday adventure. Funny, strange and easy to
   enter. This selection leans toward younger readers.

2. Science fiction: The Time Machine, H. G. Wells
   A short journey into a disturbing future. A good first book to try in
   the reader, because readers can complete it without a major commitment.

3. Detective mystery: The Hound of the Baskervilles, Arthur Conan Doyle
   Holmes, Watson, an isolated estate and an apparently supernatural threat.
   The strongest first recommendation for suspense and listening aloud.

4. Love story: The Blue Castle, L. M. Montgomery
   A woman breaks free from her controlling family and takes a chance on
   an unexpected life. Warm, witty, romantic and set in Ontario.

5. Contemporary tech thriller: Little Brother, Cory Doctorow
   A teenager fights a surveillance crackdown in modern San Francisco.
   Contemporary to its 2008 publication, not set in 2026. Includes mature
   themes and a brief sexual scene, but is not erotica. Its separate
   CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 license permits noncommercial redistribution only.

6. Surprise: The Count of Monte Cristo, Alexandre Dumas
   Betrayal, escape, a fortune and an elaborate revenge. This is the long
   adventure in the library. Its older English translation is included;
   modern copyrighted translations are not.

The six books are complete text EPUBs, not extracts. The pack preserves
original prose, including historical language, and excludes third-party
artwork. There are no accounts or online services required to read these
files. Import books/*.epub into Origami to try them now.

The files are prepared for the planned preloaded library. This research
step does not replace an existing library or change the app's current
three fictional demo entries. A starter-library migration should preserve
existing books and annotations, and should not restore a starter book
automatically after the user deletes it.
