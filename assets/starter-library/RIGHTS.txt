This pack contains six complete English text editions. It does not contain
the source editions' cover artwork, illustrations, title-page images, fonts,
logos or promotional pages. The historical book text and modern editorial
contributions have separate rights. Origami's MPL 2.0 code license does not
relicense someone else's text, translation or artwork.

REVIEWED TERRITORIES

The selected historical texts are public domain in the United States,
Canada and the United Kingdom as of this review. They can be bundled with
the app and read aloud in those territories, including in commercial
distributions. The anonymous 1846 English translation of The Count of
Monte Cristo is the selected translation. Modern commercial translations,
including Robin Buss's translation, are not included or covered here.

The book catalog pages linked in selection.json record the authors' dates
and US public-domain status. The source colophons record publication and
translation editions. Source revisions, content-file hashes and prepared
EPUB hashes are in manifest.json.

Do not describe this as a worldwide clearance. National terms differ.
Other distribution territories require local review before bundling.
Spain has a legacy term for authors who died before 7 December 1987;
The Time Machine needs that territorial check. Longer terms elsewhere
also mean a US public-domain determination alone is insufficient.
Original-author attribution and any continuing moral rights still matter.

EDITION CONTRIBUTIONS

The source editions are produced by Standard Ebooks. Their contributors
dedicate their contributions to the worldwide public domain under CC0 1.0.
The dedication applies to their own contributions; it cannot waive another
party's surviving copyright. Each source repository's LICENSE.md statement
is retained in sources/*-license.txt. The official CC0 legal text is retained
as CC0-1.0.txt. Plain-text source colophons preserve the transcription and
editorial credits. Do not imply that Standard Ebooks endorses Origami.

These are complete text editions, not photographic replicas of original
publications. Unused styling links and non-story publisher pages have been
removed. Original chapter XHTML, dedications, epigraphs, epilogues and
endnotes are retained where present. No abridgment or prose modernization
was performed. Original historical language is retained.

ARTWORK EXCLUSION

In particular, The Great Gatsby's source cover uses Francis Cugat's
Celestial Eyes, and The Count of Monte Cristo's uses Mead Schaeffer's art.
US public-domain status does not establish that those paintings can be
used in Canada or the UK. No art from any of the six source editions is
included. Origami may use its own separately licensed cover designs.
