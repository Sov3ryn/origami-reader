Origami starter library: commercial and international edition record
Reviewed 13 October 2026. Pack version 3, English.

The intended release is pay what you want, including paid copies. No
selected text has a NonCommercial-only license. Origami code remains
MPL 2.0; the book texts and edition contributions keep separate rights.
These editions are preloaded once by the app, with original local cover
illustrations dedicated under CC0 1.0. Source-edition art is not included.
Existing imports and annotations are retained. The three demo books and
their annotations are backed up locally before removal from the library.

HISTORICAL EDITIONS
All five historical texts and the selected translations were published
before 1931. Every identified original author, collaborator and translator
died before 1925. The latest death is Stoker in 1912: even an ordinary
life-plus-100-year economic term ends before 2026. The anonymous 1846
translation is much older than publication-based terms. This avoids relying
on complex shorter-term, non-revival or transitional arguments for Wells,
Doyle and Montgomery. Replacement is a conservative release decision,
not a determination that the old books are protected everywhere.

Standard Ebooks contributions are dedicated under CC0 1.0, including its
fallback license, worldwide within the rights contributors own. That cannot
waive someone else's rights. Pinned source commits, licenses and editorial
colophons are retained. Original prose, dedications, epigraphs and endnotes
remain in order. Artwork, fonts, source logos and scans are excluded.
Verne's three runic diagrams are replaced by their supplied descriptions;
the cipher's Latin transcription and decoding remain in the prose. This
text-only edition is not a facsimile. No modern introduction, modern
translation or modern publisher artwork is licensed by this review.
Do not imply Standard Ebooks endorsement.

INTERNATIONAL SCOPE AND RELEASE GATE
Economic-term review covers the US, Canada, UK, EU general term (including
Spain's older-author rule), Australia, Brazil, India, mainland China, Japan,
Mexico and Jamaica. Primary references are in international-review.json.
This is not certification under every national law. Preserve authorship
and integrity where moral rights survive economic copyright.

There is no universal public-domain-means-no-fees-everywhere assurance.
Senegal's 2008 Act, Article 157, requires declarations and royalties for
specified public-domain exploitation. WIPO also describes special-fee
systems in other countries, including Algeria; applicability to these
foreign works and current local practice needs specific review. Standard
Ebooks' CC0 cannot waive statutory national obligations.

Unreviewed countries and special-fee cases are not cleared. Release-mode
packaging requires --release --country=CODE for a reviewed destination.
A worldwide installer containing the five classics must resolve remaining
exceptions, or omit these texts in affected destinations. No automatic
geography detection or legal exemption based on the zero-price option is
claimed. The development ZIP is a prepared source pack with these notices,
not proof of unconditional worldwide clearance.

REMOVED FROM THIS SELECTION
The Enchanted Castle was replaced with Dracula at the user's request for
a more serious supernatural selection. The slot is now Gothic horror.
Little Brother is excluded because its NC license does not clear the paid
bundle. The Time Machine, The Hound of the Baskervilles and The Blue Castle
were replaced for the broader term strategy. Old source-pack files are
backed up locally, outside the public pack. User imports are untouched.
The package allowlist excludes inactive titles even if stale files remain.

LATER LANGUAGE EDITIONS
English only now. Roughly twenty languages or different books per language
remain later work. Each edition needs translator/publication/rights review.
An old original does not grant rights to a modern translation. New
translators must grant worldwide commercial distribution rights. CC BY-SA
adaptations retain their applicable terms. English hashes are not language
clearance. Book-specific credits and licenses remain separate from app MPL.
