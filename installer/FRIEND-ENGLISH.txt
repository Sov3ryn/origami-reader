ORIGAMI READER 0.1.0 BETA 5, ENGLISH

This is a private beta for friends. Please do not share or re-upload it.

ALREADY INSTALLED AN EARLIER BETA?
Uninstall it first: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Origami Reader
> Uninstall. Your library, notes, cards and settings are kept and appear again
in the new version.

INSTALL
1. Extract the entire ZIP to a local folder with a short path, for example
   C:\Origami-Setup. Extracting needs about 14 GB; once it is done you can
   delete the ZIP. The installed app needs up to 14 GB more with every model,
   and much less if you leave models out (see below).
2. Open Origami Reader Installer.exe inside the extracted folder.
   Keep bootstrap, prerequisites and payload beside the installer.
   It checks for WebView2 and Visual C++ and offers the included Microsoft
   installers if needed. A runtime installation may require a restart.
3. Choose a NEW installation folder, then the models you want (Preferences).
   Accept the app license, and the image model terms to install pictures.
4. When setup finishes, choose Close Installer and Launch Origami.
5. Open a book and press Play (or the Space bar). Change voices with the voice
   button in the player or the Voice tab.

CHOOSING MODELS (all optional, all offline)
- Light voices (about 0.8 GB): Kitten Mini, Kitten Micro and Kokoro, 12
  English voices that run on the processor. Best for laptops without a strong
  graphics card. On such computers Origami starts with Kitten Mini; switch in
  Settings, Voices and speech. The very first sentence can take a minute while
  Windows checks the new files; after that it is quick.
- Qwen voices (Set up local speech, about 2.4 GB plus the voices): more
  natural, custom voices, several languages. Best with an NVIDIA card; on the
  processor it works but is slow.
- Fast picture model, SD-Turbo (about 2.5 GB): covers and portraits in
  seconds. Powered by Stability AI.
- Book cover and portrait model, DreamShaper (about 2 GB): richer pictures,
  slower. Needs a graphics card with Vulkan drivers for good speed.
For a small laptop: light voices and SD-Turbo are a good start; you can leave
Qwen and DreamShaper out. The shared speech packages (about 4.7 GB) install
with either kind of voice.
Handwriting reading and Related notes now come with the app; nothing to
download.

NEW IN THIS BETA
- Picture cards: cover parts of a diagram, map or photo and learn what is under
  them (Work mode, ... menu > All study cards > Picture cards).
- Practice tests, exam dates that bring cards forward, and card sets you can
  share as files.
- Plans can sit inside notes; arcs and angle dimensions.
- Sticky notes on sheets, boards, plans and book pages (right-click a
  paragraph > Add a sticky note).
- Handwriting: photos of handwritten pages, unsure words underlined in orange
  with other readings offered.
- Read a note aloud; import a folder of Markdown or Obsidian notes; subjects
  inside subjects; plugin blocks (the sample adds a focus timer).
- Vaults can be unlocked from Work mode.

ALREADY IN WORK MODE (Ctrl+Shift+W switches between Read and Work)
Notes with headings, tables, callouts, [[links]], sticky notes and seven
papers; study cards (FSRS) with a Listen mode and pen answers; formulas,
diagrams, charts, spreadsheets with Excel import and export, boards and precise
plans (DXF export); templates (Ctrl+Shift+N), workspaces with export and
import, vaults; online embeds only after you allow them; plugins. Press / in a
note for blocks, Ctrl+K to search everything. Work mode opens with a short
"Getting started" tour.

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS
Windows 10/11 x64. NVIDIA speech acceleration needs a recent driver. Picture
generation needs graphics drivers with Vulkan. This is an unsigned private
beta: do not disable Windows security protections. New versions still come as
a full kit like this one.

Please report your Windows version, computer model, graphics card (if any),
which models you installed, what you tried and any error text.
