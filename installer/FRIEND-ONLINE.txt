This is a private beta for invited friends. Please do not share or re-upload it.

ALREADY INSTALLED AN EARLIER BETA?
Setup offers to replace it. Your library, notes, cards and settings are kept.

INSTALL
1. Keep this whole folder together and stay connected to the internet.
2. Run Origami Reader Installer.exe. If Windows says the app is unrecognised
   (this beta is not signed), choose More info, then Run anyway. Do not turn
   off Windows security. Setup offers the included Microsoft installers for
   WebView2 and Visual C++ if they are missing.
   If Windows says "Smart App Control blocked an app that may be unsafe",
   Origami cannot run on that computer for now: Smart App Control only
   allows signed apps and this free beta is not signed. Please do not reset
   your computer for it.
3. Choose a new installation folder, your languages, the language of your
   starter books and appearance. Setup picks the models for your computer
   and four voices per language, to keep the download small. Every other
   voice and model can be downloaded later in the app (Voices, and
   Settings, Models).
   The review page shows how much setup will download. Only what you choose
   is downloaded, each part from its publisher, checked before use.
4. Accept the app license (and the image model licenses for pictures).
5. When setup finishes, choose Launch Origami.

For a small laptop: keep the light voices setup suggests.
If a download stops, run setup again: finished downloads are kept.

The full guide and the feedback page are on the private Origami page where
you downloaded this.
