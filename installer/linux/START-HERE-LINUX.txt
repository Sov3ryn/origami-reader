Origami Reader, private beta for Linux
======================================

Thank you for testing! This is a private beta shared only with trusted
testers. Please do not pass it on.

What you get
------------
- The full reader: library, book mode (two pages, page turns, the opening
  from the cover), notes, bookmarks, the offline author notes and portraits,
  language flags, imports (EPUB, PDF, Kindle, Word, text and more).
- Local speech with 20 English voices (Qwen3-TTS 0.6B). It uses an NVIDIA
  card when there is one, and the processor otherwise (slower).
- Optional local artwork for covers and voice portraits (DreamShaper 8 LCM).

Nothing is sent anywhere while you read. The installer itself downloads build
tools and Python packages once, from their official sources.

Origami Reader is made by Sov3ryn. It is free to use at home, school or work;
copies may not be shared, sold or modified. The installer asks you to accept
the full terms in LICENSE.txt first (running it with --yes counts as accepting).

Install
-------
1. Extract this folder somewhere in your home folder (not on a USB stick).
2. Open a terminal in the folder and run:

       ./install-linux.sh

   It explains each step and asks before using sudo or downloading
   anything. Building takes roughly 10 to 30 minutes the first time,
   depending on the computer and the connection.
3. Open "Origami Reader" from your applications menu.

Needs: a 64-bit computer, about 14 GB of free space, an internet connection
during the install, and Python 3.10 to 3.13 (most current distributions have
it). Tested distributions: see the notes from the sender. Debian/Ubuntu,
Fedora and Arch are supported by the script; others need the packages listed
at https://v2.tauri.app/start/prerequisites/ installed first.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
Everything is logged to ~/origami-install.log. Please send that file, plus:
- your distribution and version, and your graphics card,
- what you clicked and what you expected.
Inside the app, a short note of what happened and when is very helpful too.

Remove
------
    ~/.local/share/origami-reader/uninstall-origami.sh

Your books, notes and settings stay unless you add --remove-library.
