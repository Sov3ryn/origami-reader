Origami light reader: license and source
=========================================

light_tts.py in this folder is the light reader, a separate program that
Origami Reader starts to read aloud with the Kitten and Kokoro speech models.

It is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at
your option) any later version: see LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt. Its complete source
is light_tts.py itself, a plain Python file, which you may copy, change and
share under that license. Copyright (c) 2026 Sov3ryn.

Why a separate license: the light reader uses phonemizer (phonemizer-fork) and
espeak-ng, which are GPL-3. The rest of Origami Reader is under its own terms
(LICENSE and APP-LICENSE.md). The light reader runs as its own process and
talks to the app only through one JSON message per line on standard input and
output. It shares no code with the rest of Origami.

GPL components the light reader uses, with their source code:

- phonemizer-fork 3.3.2 (GPL-3.0-or-later)
  https://pypi.org/project/phonemizer-fork/3.3.2/#files
  https://github.com/bootphon/phonemizer
- espeak-ng, as packaged by espeakng-loader 0.2.4 (espeak-ng is GPL-3.0-or-later)
  https://pypi.org/project/espeakng-loader/0.2.4/#files
  https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng

Packaging requirement: any Origami kit that includes these components must
also include their corresponding source archives (or a written offer for them,
valid for three years), not only these links. See docs/LIGHT-READER-LICENSING.md.
