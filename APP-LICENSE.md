## Privacy

Origami Reader works on your computer. It has no account, no ads, no analytics
and no tracking. Your books, notes, recordings, voices and settings stay on
your computer, and the AI models run there too, unless you connect an online
service yourself (see "Online services you connect").

The app connects to the internet only:

- to check for and download updates, from GitHub (Sov3ryn/origami-updates),
  when automatic updates are on or you check yourself;
- to download a model, voice or Studio part you choose, from its publisher
  (Hugging Face, GitHub, Stability AI) or from the Origami voices repository;
- when you open a link, allow embeds in Work mode, or open the support page;
- to send an online service you connect yourself what you ask it to process.

Setup also downloads the parts you choose from their publishers, and Python
packages from PyPI and PyTorch (and AMD, on computers with an AMD graphics
card). Those services see your computer's network address and what is
downloaded, under their own privacy policies. Sov3ryn receives none of this,
apart from the download counts GitHub shows for each release. If you send
feedback or a problem report, you choose what it contains.

If you donate, Ko-fi and its payment providers handle your payment under their
own terms. Sov3ryn receives what Ko-fi shares (such as your name, email and
message) and uses it only to thank you and to unlock your thank-you items.
